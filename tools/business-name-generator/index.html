💡 Tips for Choosing a Business Name

🔍

Check Domain Availability

After picking a name, search for it on GoDaddy or Namecheap to see if the .com domain is available.

📱

Keep It Short

Names under 12 characters are easier to remember, type and brand. Avoid hyphens and numbers.

🌍

Easy to Pronounce

If people can't say it, they can't recommend it. Test it by saying it out loud to a friend.

™️

Check for Trademarks

Before finalizing, search the name on your country's trademark registry to avoid legal issues.

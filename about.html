Our Story

AI Biz Tools was built with one simple belief: everyone deserves access to powerful AI tools, regardless of their budget or technical background. We saw too many great tools locked behind expensive subscriptions and decided to change that.

We started with a handful of career tools — a resume generator and cover letter writer — and quickly grew into a full suite of free AI tools used by thousands of people every month around the world.

Every tool is designed to be simple enough for a beginner yet powerful enough for professionals. We keep everything free by focusing on quality, not quantity, and continuously improving based on your feedback.

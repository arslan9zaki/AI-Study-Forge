Overview

AI Biz Tools ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data.

We may collect basic information such as your name and email address when you contact us or subscribe to our newsletter. Our AI tools process the text you enter solely to generate your requested output — this input is not stored on our servers.

How We Use Your Information

Collected information is used only to respond to your inquiries, deliver newsletters you have opted into, and improve the quality of our tools and content. We do not sell or rent your personal data to any third party.

API Keys & Local Storage

If any of our tools allow you to save an API key, that key is stored exclusively in your browser's local storage on your own device. It is never transmitted to our servers or shared with any third party.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies to improve user experience and analyze site traffic through tools such as Google Analytics. You can disable cookies at any time through your browser settings without affecting the core functionality of our tools.

We use third-party services including Google Fonts, Google Analytics, and Web3Forms for contact and newsletter submissions. Each of these services operates under their own privacy policies.

Data Protection

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration or destruction.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal data to us, please contact us immediately.
